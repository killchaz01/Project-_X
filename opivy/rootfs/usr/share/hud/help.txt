OP IVY HELP

THE MENU BAR (top)
  OP IVY        system menu: About, System Settings, App Store, Force Quit,
                Sleep, Restart, Shut Down, Lock Screen, Log Out
  APP NAME      the app in front. Click for Hide, Quit, Zoom, Full Screen
  FILE EDIT VIEW WINDOW HELP
                menus for the app in front
  NET BT VOL    network, Bluetooth, volume (scroll on VOL to change it)
  SEARCH        find apps and files (or press Cmd+Space)
  CLOCK         click for notifications and Do Not Disturb

THE DOCK (bottom)
  Click an app to open it or bring it to the front.
  A mark under an app means it is running.
  Drag an icon onto another to reorder. Right-click for more.
  Drop files on the Trash to delete them, or on an app to open them.

THE DESKTOP
  Every panel of the console is live. Click one to use it.
  Files in your Desktop folder appear at the top right.
  Double-click to open, right-click for more.

YOUR APPS
  Grid icon in the dock, or Cmd+Shift+A     Application Center
  Files > Applications                      every app, as a folder
  Cmd+Space                                 search by name

KEYS (Cmd is the Windows key on a PC keyboard)
  Cmd+Space          search            Cmd+Q      quit the app
  Cmd+W              close window      Cmd+H      hide the app
  Cmd+M              minimise          Cmd+Tab    switch windows
  Cmd+C / X / V / A  copy, cut, paste, select all
  Cmd+,              Control Center    Cmd+Return terminal
  Cmd+Shift+3 / 4 / 5   screenshots    Cmd+Option+Esc  Force Quit
  Ctrl+Cmd+F         full screen       Ctrl+Cmd+Q lock
  Ctrl+Space         next keyboard layout

SETTINGS
  Control Center (CONTROL in the bar, or Cmd+,): themes, dock, desktop,
  keyboard layout, language and time zone.

INSTALL TO A DISK
  Open Terminal and run:   sudo install-to-disk
