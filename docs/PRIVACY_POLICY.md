# EverStrong Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

EverStrong ("the app") is made by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Summary
**We do not collect, store, sell or share any of your personal data.** Everything you enter stays on your device.

## Information stored on your device
The app stores the following, only on your iPhone or iPad:
- Your first name and fitness focus
- Medicines, reminder times and when you marked them taken
- Family members' names, phone numbers, relationships and photos you choose
- Check-ins and completed workouts

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. If your device backup (iCloud or computer) is turned on, it may be included in that backup, which Apple's privacy policy covers. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Camera
Form Check uses your camera to detect body position in real time using Apple's on-device Vision technology. Video is processed live, and it is **never recorded, saved or uploaded**.

## Messages and phone calls
When you send a check-in or call a family member, the app opens your iPhone's own Messages or Phone app. These messages and calls are handled by Apple and your mobile carrier, not by EverStrong.

## Notifications
Medicine and check-in reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

## Analytics, advertising and tracking
The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Health disclaimer
EverStrong provides general fitness information and reminders. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.
